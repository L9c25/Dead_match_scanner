==============================================================================

1. AUTOMATIC INSTALLATION (1 CLICK):
   - Double-click the file: “Instalar_Deadlock_Scanner.bat”
   - The installer will copy the files to the Adobe Premiere Pro extensions folder
     and enable the necessary permissions in the Windows Registry.

2. OPENING IN PREMIERE PRO:
   - Open or restart Adobe Premiere Pro (versions 2025 or 2026).
   - In the top menu, go to: Window > Extensions.
   - Click on: “Deadlock Toolkit”.

3. LOCAL ASSETS PACKAGE (FREE PLAN):
   - Extract the “Deadlock_Assets” folder you downloaded from Google Drive to
     any location on your computer (e.g., Documents or C:\Deadlock_Assets).
   - In the extension panel in Premiere Pro, click the folder icon (📁)
     or the “Configure Folder” banner and select the “Deadlock_Assets” folder.
   - Done! The panel will instantly detect all 280+ items and icons.
